IN THE ROLEPLAY ROOM

Roleplay tips.

Practical reminders for preparing, presenting, and thinking through your response.

01How to Begin+
Always begin with a professional yet genuine introduction that feels true to you and reflects your positive personality! It is best that you don’t spend too much of your prep time dwelling on your introduction, thus why it is ideal to structure a good introduction that at least includes your name, role, and the judge’s role from beforehand.
02Incorporating PIs+
Bringing up PIs can sometimes feel unnatural with your solution. Hence why it is best to do what works for you, whether that be centering your Roleplay around the PIs, using them as a Table of Contents, or involving them as subcategories of your solution. But remember that PIs are the most important thing to cover!
03What to Do When You're Stuck+
Thinking of a Roleplay as a natural conversation for expressing creative business ideas you hold in your mind as opposed to a forced business meeting can help the situation flow more naturally. Whenever you feel like you “messed up”, remember there is no such thing in a conversation. The judge won’t know you messed up unless you directly address it, because there is no script to follow…they are your ideas!
